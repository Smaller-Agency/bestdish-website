Brownie Bites

Nine mini brownies, baked in Bunner's own kitchen. Rich, moist, and completely delicious — no animal products, no gluten, no nuts. Sized for a quick chocolate fix, waiting in your building.

Chef

Bunner's Bakeshop

Serving

1 brownie (25 g)

Category

Sweet

The chef

"No matter what diet or allergy you live with, everyone deserves the option to eat something that makes them feel safe."— Bunner's Bakeshop, Bunner's Bakeshop

Heating instructions

Serve

Keep frozen. Let thaw at room temperature for 10–15 minutes before serving.

Warm

For a gooey centre, microwave one brownie for 10–15 seconds.

Keep frozen until use. Do not refreeze after thawing.

Ingredients

Bunner's all-purpose flour blend (chickpea flour, sorghum flour, fava bean flour, potato starch, tapioca starch), Cocoa, Salt, Baking powder, Xanthan gum, Canola oil, Sugar, Sucanat, Applesauce, Vanilla, Vegan chocolate chips (sugar, unsweetened chocolate, cocoa butter, soy lecithin, vanilla).

Contains: Soy.

May contain: Nothing — baked on dedicated gluten-free, nut-free lines.

Made in Canada with domestic and imported ingredients.

From the restaurant

Bunner's Bakeshop.

Since 2010, Bunner's Bakeshop has been proving that vegan and gluten-free don't require compromise — traditional baked goods indistinguishable from their classic counterparts, made in a dedicated nut-free kitchen.
